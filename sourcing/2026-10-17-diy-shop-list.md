# DIY shop list, 17 October 2026

These are the parts still open on the build after everything received up to 17 October. It covers the rest of the Hornbach sheet in [bom-shopping-list.md](bom-shopping-list.md), plus two items that depend on checks at home. Each item is described by what it has to be rather than by a Hornbach article number, so any DIY shop will do. Each item links to the Hornbach product page the list priced, so you can see a picture of what to look for. For the glue, the link is the Ponal Classic 120 g you already bought. The "Hornbach €" column is the price the list has for comparison: 49,22 € for the ten core items.

## Check at home before going

- **Second MDF piece:** the receipt says 680 x 880 mm, and the Ø 860 disc needs 860 x 860. If it really is 680, add item 11.
- **Glue:** the bottle bought is 120 g of Ponal Classic (D2); the list had planned 225 g. Get a second bottle if the panel joints look like they'll use more than that.
- **M2.5 standoffs for the display:** they should be in the Waveshare box. A DIY shop is unlikely to stock them; BerryBase sells a 4-pack for 1-2 €.
