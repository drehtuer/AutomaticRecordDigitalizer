This revision: the frame is a glued box of 18 mm birch plywood — side and end panels with window cut-outs, open at the carousel end — and only the two X rails ride on 2040 extrusion bolted along the top edges, with one extrusion tie across the open end; about 4 m of extrusion instead of 10, no diagonals, and the brush, cue-servo and camera brackets simply screw to the panels. The carousel is a plywood disc that is nothing but a floor: it rolls on ten printed brackets with 608 bearings around a central 6005 bearing, is indexed by a stepper through a printed GT2 ring in eight segments, and the printed hub and rim segments hold the 24 combs at their exact angles, so no wood cut has to be precise.
